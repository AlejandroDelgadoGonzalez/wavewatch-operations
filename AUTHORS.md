## Joint authorship statement

The project was created through paired collaboration. Both authors participated in product decisions, operational analysis, scenario validation, testing and AI-assisted implementation. Because the initial development was completed together on a shared computer and GitHub account, the early commit history records the account used to save the work and should not be interpreted as exclusive authorship.

Future commits should name both authors with Git's `Co-authored-by` metadata when a change is produced jointly. Work completed independently should be committed under the account of the person who completed it.
